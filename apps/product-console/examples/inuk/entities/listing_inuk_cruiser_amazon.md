:::richtext{#description region="content" policy="local-edit" title="Product description"}
The INUK CRUISER_WR Backpack is a 25L water-resistant daypack designed for people who move between city routines, short trips and light outdoor plans. With a 290 x 460 x 150 mm body, 0.8 kg weight and up to 15-inch laptop fit, it offers more carrying power than a small commuter pack while keeping a clean daily look. The organized pocket layout helps separate laptop, documents, water bottle, umbrella, chargers, passport, wallet and travel essentials. Use it for rainy commutes, school, cafe work, airports, weekend getaways and soft outdoor days when capacity and weather protection both matter.
