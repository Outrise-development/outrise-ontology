:::richtext{#description region="content" policy="local-edit" title="Product description"}
Designed for daily commutes, campus life and weekend trips, the INUK CRUISER combines a spacious 25L interior with organized storage, laptop protection and a water-resistant exterior.

Key Features

Spacious 25L capacity holds a laptop, notebooks, jacket, lunch box, water bottle, umbrella, chargers and short-trip essentials.

Padded laptop compartment fits most laptops up to 15 inches.

Main compartment, front quick-access pocket, side bottle pockets and back zipper pocket help keep essentials organized.

Water-resistant polyester exterior helps protect belongings from light rain and everyday splashes.

Adjustable shoulder straps provide a stable carry for commuting, campus days and weekend travel.

Back zipper pocket keeps passports, cards and other small valuables closer to the body.

Outdoor-inspired functionality meets a clean design suitable for everyday city use.

Ideal For

Daily commuting, college, café work, short business trips, airports, road trips, weekend getaways and light outdoor activities.

Specifications

Product dimensions: 18.1 × 11.4 × 5.9 in / 46 × 29 × 15 cm

Capacity: 25L

Product weight: 1.76 lb / 0.8 kg

Material: Polyester

Laptop compatibility: Fits most laptops up to 15 in

Closure: Zipper

Bag type: Laptop backpack / commuter daypack

Country of origin: China

Available colors: Black, Sage Leaf, Misty Purple

Warranty: 1 year

Care Instructions

Spot clean gently with a soft, damp cloth. Allow the backpack to air dry completely. Do not machine wash, bleach or tumble dry.

Please Note

The exterior is water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It is not designed for prolonged exposure to heavy rain or submersion. Please review the measurements before purchasing. Product color may vary slightly due to lighting and screen settings.
