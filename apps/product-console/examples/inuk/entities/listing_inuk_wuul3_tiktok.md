The INUK WUUL is a lightweight 21L backpack designed for work, school, city routines and short trips, with organized storage, laptop space and travel-friendly details.

Key Features

21L capacity provides practical room for a laptop, books, water bottle, umbrella, light layers, chargers and everyday essentials.

Dedicated storage fits most laptops up to 14 inches.

Multiple interior and exterior pockets keep frequently used items organized and easy to reach.

Water-resistant polyester exterior helps protect daily essentials from light rain and splashes.

A luggage pass-through strap lets the backpack rest securely on a suitcase handle.

Back zipper pocket keeps a passport, cards and other small valuables close to the body.

Lightweight construction makes it comfortable for commuting, campus, airports and everyday movement.

Ideal For

Work, college, commuting, café visits, city walks, weekend trips, personal-item travel and light outdoor use.

Specifications

Product dimensions: 16.5 × 11.0 × 4.9 in / 42 × 28 × 12.5 cm

Capacity: 21L

Product weight: 1.34 lb / 0.61 kg

Material: Polyester

Laptop compatibility: Fits most laptops up to 14 in

Closure: Zipper

Bag type: Laptop backpack / casual daypack

Country of origin: China

Available colors: Black, Milky Moka, Dalia Orange, Apricot Brandy

Warranty: 1 year

Care Instructions

Spot clean gently with a soft, damp cloth and allow to air dry. Do not machine wash, bleach or tumble dry.

Please Note

The exterior is water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It helps protect against light rain and everyday splashes but is not intended for heavy rain or submersion. Please check the measurements and laptop dimensions before ordering.
