## Short trip. Hands free.

Pack your clothes and everyday essentials, then put your trip on your back. NAVI's 38L main compartment and suitcase-style opening make it easy to see what you packed. Internal clothing straps and mesh pockets help keep your essentials organized.

### Move through the day

Padded shoulder straps, a cushioned back panel and grab handles give you ways to carry the bag as your plans change. A concealed side zip pocket keeps small essentials out of sight; side buckle straps add another fastening across the main compartment.

### From city streets to outdoor stops

The water-resistant, 900D coated recycled polyester body helps handle light rain and everyday splashes. A padded sleeve provides space for a laptop up to 16 inches, with fit depending on the device dimensions. A luggage pass-through is available for trips when you bring a suitcase too.

- Dimensions: 485 H × 320 W × 200 D mm
- Weight: 1.2 kg
- Color: Black Forest
